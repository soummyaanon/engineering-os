    What grade is your agent setup?

    Lighthouse for coding agents. Grades guardrails, working rules, skills and feedback loops, then tells you the biggest wins. Read-only, nothing leaves your machine, works without installing anything.

    $ curl -fsSL engineering-os-hazel.vercel.app/score | python3 -
    # after installing
$ eos score --card     # shareable 1200×630 card
$ eos score --badge    # README badge with your grade
$ eos receipts         # what the guards stopped this week

    Playbooks that route to the best skills

    /eos:backend

    /eos:reverse
